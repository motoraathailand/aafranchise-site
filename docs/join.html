Why 14 days?

Google Play lets us release an app only after at least 12 testers have stayed opted in for 14 days in a row. Every tester counts, so please don’t opt out or uninstall early.

Troubleshooting

Google Play says “App not found” or “Item not found”

This almost always means Play does not know you are a tester yet. Check that you joined the group (step 1), tapped “Become a tester” (step 3) and that Play on your phone uses the same Google account. After that it can take a few minutes, sometimes a few hours, before the install page works. Close Google Play, wait a little and open the install link again.

I am signed in to the wrong Google account

On your phone, open Google Play, tap your profile picture and pick the account you used for the group. In your browser, tap the profile picture at the top right and switch to that account. If you already used the wrong one, repeat steps 1 and 3 with the right account.

I can’t join the group, or it asks for approval

Tap “Ask to join” and wait for approval. If nothing happens after a day, email motoraathailand@gmail.com with your Gmail address. Make sure you are signed in to Google, and open this page in Chrome: Google Groups often fails inside other apps.

The opt-in page says I can’t become a tester

Testing may not be open yet (see the status at the top of this page), or the account is not in the group yet. Finish step 1 with the same account, wait a few minutes and reload the page.

This page opened inside Facebook, LINE or Instagram and nothing works

Tap the three dots and choose “Open in Chrome”, or use the “Open in Chrome” button at the top of this page.

The install button is missing or says my device isn’t compatible

AA Franchise needs an Android phone or tablet with Android 7 or newer and Google Play. It can’t be installed on iPhone. Work or school accounts can block it, so use a personal Gmail account.

If it doesn’t work

No problem. Start again from step 1. If the install page isn’t ready yet, wait a moment first.

Questions

Which devices work?

Android phones and tablets with Android 7 or newer and Google Play.

Do I need internet or an account in the app?

No account or sign-in. Franchise data works offline; only the optional AI chat needs internet and your own AI key.

Does the test version have ads?

No. The closed-test version has no ads at all. After the official launch there will be small banner ads only.

Where can I test it?

Thailand only. AA Franchise and its test are for Thailand, so you need a Google account, Google Play and a Thai Play Store country.

Found a problem or have feedback?

Email motoraathailand@gmail.com, or use “Send feedback” in the app.
