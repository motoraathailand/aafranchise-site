Questions

Is AA Franchise free?

Yes. The test version has no ads at all, and the released app shows small banner ads only (never full-screen ads).

Where does the franchise data come from?

From ThaiFranchiseCenter public pages. Each field keeps its source page and date, plus the raw text. It may be out of date, so confirm with the brand owner.

Do I need to sign in?

No. The app has no account and no Google sign-in. Everything you save stays on your phone, and you can delete it all in Settings.

Do I need an AI key?

Only for the AI chat. Get a free Gemini key (required for chat), and optionally Groq and OpenRouter keys. They stay on your phone. Everything else works without a key.

How do I join the test?

Tap “Get the app” and follow the 5 steps on the join page: join the Google Group, use the same Google account as your phone, open the Play test link, install, and keep it for 14 days.

Why a test instead of a normal download?

AA Franchise is in a Google Play closed test before it opens to everyone. Google asks for at least 12 testers to stay opted in for 14 days. Testers help us find problems first.

Which phones and countries?

Android phones and tablets with Android 7 or newer and Google Play. The app and the test are for Thailand only.

Which languages?

Thai, English, Korean, Chinese and Hindi. AA Franchise follows your phone language and falls back to English. The globe button is on every screen.
